# YanolTech design reference

Reference: [yanoltech.com](https://yanoltech.com/) (inspected Oct 2026).

## Confirmed

- **Brand:** YanolTech (unchanged per project requirement).
- **Body background:** `#F7F3EE` (cream paper).
- **Primary accent:** `#0F86A1` (teal).
- **Typography:** Plus Jakarta Sans.
- **Hero:** Teal-to-peach gradient with `/circle/hero.svg` texture; line-art hero illustration; CTAs “Launch Project” (peach pill) and “Book a Demo” (white pill).
- **Navigation:** Services, Ecosystem, Projects, Pricing, About Us (dropdown), Start a Project, Blog, Careers, UK flag, Contact Us pill.
- **Sections:** Partner marquee, growth stats card, What We Do services, headline stats, sectors illustration, portfolio, ecosystem, testimonials, teal CTA band, footer, Yanol Assistant chat widget.

## Assumptions

- Some portfolio cards load dynamically on the live SPA; static representative cards are used until a CMS/API is connected.
- Inner pages follow the same shell and tokens; long-form copy mirrors public positioning, not verbatim legal text from production.
